A small Cleveland roaster built around thoughtful sourcing, close attention, and coffee worth returning to.

A Cleveland Original

Small by choice.
Focused by design.

City Roast pioneered small-batch, craft-roasted coffee in Cleveland more than two decades ago. From the beginning, our goal was simple: offer an alternative to coffee roasted for mass production rather than for flavor.

We remain a small, family-run business by choice. It lets us take time with each customer and be more selective about the coffees we buy and roast.

Our coffees are estate grown, carefully chosen, and roasted in small batches to bring out their distinct character. The result is coffee for the people and places that care about what is in the cup.
